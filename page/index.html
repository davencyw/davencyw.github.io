About

I am a research engineer working mostly on ML and GPUs. The part of the work I enjoy most is taking a method from a paper and making it real: reading the math, finding where the time actually goes, and writing the kernel or pipeline that makes it practical.

Over the years I have led teams working on sub second human skeletal pose tracking, multi modal pose optimization and action recognition. Most of my time goes into C++, Python and CUDA, and into keeping those systems maintainable once they leave the lab.

This page is where I write things down: short technical logs on whatever I am digging into, and longer write ups of side projects.
